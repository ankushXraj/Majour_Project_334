About FlowerCycle

Honoring devotion while protecting our environment.

FlowerCycle is a digital waste-to-worth management platform designed to coordinate temples, waste collection teams, and eco-product processing units through one unified workflow.

!

Why Temple Flower Waste Matters

Every day, tons of offered flowers end up in landfills or water bodies alongside plastic wrappers and threads—causing water pollution, algae growth, and rotting organic waste.

✓

Coordinated Collection & Conversion

Temples schedule pickups in seconds, collection teams track and transport batches cleanly, and processing units segregate petals to craft incense sticks, organic compost, natural colours, and decorative items.

How It Works

Six connected steps from altar to eco-product.

A transparent, end-to-end digital lifecycle built for temples, collectors, and sustainability teams.

Step 01
🛕

Temple Registration

Temples register on the platform with their contact details and pickup location to join the circular network.

Step 02
📋

Waste Collection Request

Temples submit flower waste collection requests specifying flower type, estimated weight, and preferred pickup date.

Step 03
🚛

Waste Collection

Collection teams view assigned temple requests in real time, pick up the floral waste, and update collection status.

Step 04
🌼

Waste Segregation

Collected batches are weighed and separated into usable flower petals and non-floral materials like plastic or cloth.

Step 05
🪔

Eco-Product Manufacturing

Segregated flowers are transformed into natural incense sticks, organic compost, herbal colours, and decorative products.

Step 06
🌍

Sustainable Impact

Environmental pollution is reduced, inventory is tracked digitally, and valuable eco-friendly products return to the community.
